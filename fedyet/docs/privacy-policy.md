# Fed Yet – Privacy Policy

Last updated: [DATE OF PUBLICATION]

Fed Yet helps a household keep track of pet care. This policy explains what the app stores, where,
and why. Contact: [YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL]

## What we store

**Your home's care records.** The names you give your home, yourself, your pets and their tasks;
when each task was done and by which member of the home; supplies and amounts you enter.

- If you use the app on one phone only, this stays on that phone.
- If you share a home, it is stored in Google Cloud Firestore (Firebase) so every member's phone
  can show it. Only members of that home can read it.

**An anonymous ID.** To share a home, each phone gets a random ID from Firebase Authentication.
We do not ask for your email, phone number or real name. The name you type can be a nickname.

**Pet photos** you add stay on the phone where you added them. They are not uploaded.

## Location (optional)

The walk check needs to know roughly where you walk. If you choose **Use my location**, the app
reads the phone's approximate location once. If you type a town instead, no location is read.
To get the weather, the app sends coordinates rounded to about 1 km to our weather service, which
passes them on to MET Norway (the Norwegian Meteorological Institute). The place is kept on your
phone only.

## Ads

The app shows a banner ad on two screens. Ads are provided by Google AdMob. Before any ad is
requested, Google's consent form asks for your choice where the law requires it (for example in the
EU, the UK and some US states). You can change your choice at any time under
**Home → Privacy choices**. Google's use of data for ads is described at
https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads

## What we do not do

We do not sell your data. We do not use analytics. We do not read your contacts, photos (other than
one you pick for a pet), or messages.

## Notifications

Reminders are worked out on your phone. They are only shown when a task is due and nobody in the
home has done it.

## Keeping and deleting data

- Care history on the phone is kept for 90 days.
- In a shared home, the owner can delete the home for everyone: **Home → Delete this home**.
  Other members can leave it: **Home → Leave this home**.
- Uninstalling the app removes everything stored on the phone.
- To ask for deletion of anything else, write to [YOUR_CONTACT_EMAIL].

## Children

Fed Yet is not directed at children under 13.
