# Google Play listing – draft

**App name (30 max):** Fed Yet: Shared Pet Care Log  (28)

**Short description (80 max):**
Did someone feed the dog? One board the whole home shares. Any pet, free.  (73)

**Full description:**

Did someone already feed the dog? Was the cat's tablet given? Fed Yet answers in one glance.

ONE BOARD FOR THE WHOLE HOME
Every pet, every task, and who did it. Tap once when it's done, and everyone at home sees it on
their phone straight away.

A WARNING BEFORE ANYTHING IS DONE TWICE
Tap a meal that was already given and Fed Yet asks first. For medicine it asks you to confirm, so
nobody gives a second dose by mistake.

REMINDERS ONLY WHEN THEY MATTER
A reminder comes only when something is due and nobody has done it. If someone else already did
it, your phone stays quiet.

IS IT OK TO WALK NOW?
The walk check reads the forecast and estimates pavement heat, so you know when the ground is too
hot for paws, and when the next good time is.

EVERY ANIMAL
Dogs, cats, rabbits, guinea pigs, hamsters, birds, fish, reptiles, horses, chickens. Daily tasks,
every few days, monthly or yearly.

NEVER RUN OUT
Link food or medicine to a task and Fed Yet counts down the days left.

FREE, WITH NO LIMITS
Unlimited pets and unlimited people. No account, no email. Your history is always yours to export.

Also: home-screen widget, upcoming view for the next 30 days, spreadsheet export.

**Category:** Lifestyle (alternative: House & Home)

**Tags/keywords to use in the description naturally:** pet care tracker, dog feeding log, shared pet
schedule, pet medication reminder, family pet care, cat feeding, reptile feeding
