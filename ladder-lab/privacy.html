PLC Start does not collect, send or share any personal data. There is no account, no advert and no tracking.

What the app stores, and where

  • Your progress (finished lessons, passed tasks, quiz scores), the programs and circuits you build, and your settings (theme, zoom) are saved only on your own device, in the storage of the app or of your browser.
  • This data is never sent to me or to anyone else. I cannot see it.
  • "Export my work" writes a backup file to a place you choose. That file stays with you.

What the app does not do

  • No account and no login.
  • No adverts.
  • No analytics, no tracking, no advertising ID, no code of other companies that collects data.
  • The Android app asks for no permission. It has no access to the internet, your location, contacts, camera, microphone or files. It only opens the save or open screen of the phone when you export or import a backup.

The web version

The web version works the same way: everything is saved in your browser, and all files (fonts included) come from the same website. Like every website, the hosting company that serves the pages may keep a short technical log (IP address, time, page asked for) to run and protect the server. I do not use these logs to identify you.

How to delete your data

  • Android: uninstall the app, or use Settings → Apps → PLC Start → Storage → Clear storage.
  • Web: clear the site data of the page in your browser.

Because nothing is stored outside your device, there is nothing for me to delete.

Children

PLC Start is a training tool for students and adults. It is not made for children under 13, and it collects no data from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new text is put on this page with a new date. If a later version of the app ever needs data, this page and the store listing will say so before that version is released.
